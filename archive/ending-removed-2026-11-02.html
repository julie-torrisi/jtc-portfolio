Design upstream of AI

AI and product didn’t share a language. I built one.

With backing from the Engineering Director of Public Comments, I ran workshops that turned real behavior into inputs the ranking models train on, then gave the creator journey (start, initiate, explore, grow, make it) entry and exit criteria for every stage.

Decision traceability

Every pixel defensible. Every decision traceable.

Leadership didn’t have to take the redesign on taste. Every change traces to a job it serves: safety, clarity or control. It set the standard past comments: Feed followed the new comment action row when they simplified the action row under every post.

Removed chat bubbles · ClarityGrey bubbles added visual weight without information. Removing them lets content lead.
Unified the byline · ClarityName, time and badges sat in different places. One line scans in a single pass.
Larger comment text · ClarityComments are the product. A bigger type size pushes UI chrome into the background.
Profile size shows nesting depth · ClarityReplies shrink as the thread nests. Hierarchy without extra UI.
Tightened the stack · ClarityMore comments per screen, less scrolling.
Discoverable reply thread · ClarityNested replies now read as part of the conversation.
Like at the right edge · ControlReactions belong under the thumb.
One dominant reaction · SafetyOne dominant reaction per comment, with a tap through to every reaction and the people you know who reacted.

What I didDesigned every one of these changes and wrote the rationale leaders used to approve them.
